Search & Discover on the Stage

Home is becoming a stage with a strip. Search is still the flat page it was before: a field, a keyboard, chips in three rows and a grid. This board asks how Search and Discover should look once they sit beside the new Home, using what the other Apple TV and Android TV apps did with the same problem. It builds on the 4 October board (where S1 Native Search and Library L1 came from), adds this round's research, and lays out four page models, the smaller pieces that fit any of them, a recommendation and the calls only you can make.

Starting point

Home and Search no longer look like the same app

Both frames come from the home-stage clone on the FA87 simulator (5 October, rail set to Always Visible).

Home, Stage layout. Ambient wash, logo, meta line, synopsis, art on the right, one row at a time, the next heading peeking.

Search today (S1, merged 5 October). Black page, Recent chips, Discover as three chip rows over a grid.

What the Search page has today

  • The system search field on the results scroll view, inside the tab's own NavigationStack. Results update 350 ms after each letter. Old rows hold until the new ones land, 1 s at most. Saved to Recent when a result is opened.
  • Results are one row per add-on catalog, in fan-out order. No merge by id, no ranking, no type grouping, no people. The one loading state is "Searching…"; four empty reasons collapse into "No results."
  • Discover under the field while the query is empty: a header, type chips, catalog chips (only with more than one catalog), genre chips, then an adaptive poster grid. Only the catalog is remembered; the genre is not.
  • Add-on catalogs only. TMDB discover exists in shared code, but only for collection folders.
  • No wash, no backdrop, no stage. Nothing on this page reacts to the focused title.

What the Stage brought to Home

  • Geometry. At Medium posters the stage is 520 pt and the strip 560 pt. Large: 447 / 633, logo slot 110, four synopsis lines.
  • A swap rule. 450 ms pause after the last focus report, text fades out over 150 ms and in over 200 ms, art cross-fades in 300 ms. No swap during a page glide.
  • The ambient wash: a 160×90 blurred copy of the focused backdrop, saturated and tinted by the poster colour, cross-faded in 0.4 s. Off-switch in Home Screen settings.
  • The strip: one row per page, 0.5 s glide, the peeking row at 60 % opacity, only two rows mounted either side of focus.
  • The rail: 84 pt pill, 300 pt open, 116 pt reserved from the bezel, so content starts at 176 pt instead of 140.
  • A folder page that already runs a stage and a strip over any set of rows (FEAT-43). That is the piece Discover can borrow.
Research, round two

What this round adds to the 4 October board

The 4 October board already covered the big apps at a high level and chose S1. This round went deeper on the stage question and newer, into mid-2026. Only the facts that change a design decision are here; the full reports are in the sources.

Nobody runs a system keyboard over a stage

VortX, the one Stremio-style tvOS app with a living backdrop, uses it on Discover, Library and every category grid, and keeps Search a flat system-field page. Orivio stages only Home. Netflix expands the tile in place. Jellyfin Android TV paints the focused result's backdrop behind its search rows and nothing else. Google's Immersive List, the official Android TV stage-and-strip component, is documented for featured content, not search.

VortX src · Orivio src · Jellyfin src · Android TV design docs

Apple's model: field on top, browse underneath

WWDC26's search session names the Apple TV app as the example: a search tab that lands on a page with the field at the top and "the various genres and categories available before searching" under it. Apple Music on tvOS 26 adds a scope toggle under the keyboard ("Your Library") and category tiles before typing. Genius Browse (tvOS 26.4) shows a row of related titles under whichever title you focus.

WWDC26 292 · Apple TV user guide tvOS 26/27 · Apple Music guide

Search became the browse entry everywhere

Netflix removed Categories from its menu and put the genre list on the Search page's left side. Google TV opens Search onto an "Explore genres" tile grid. HBO Max's Categories page is two rows of tiles, brands then genres. Prime's Find page offers genres and collections before typing. Official NuvioTV lets Discover live Off, In Search, or as its own sidebar item.

Netflix help · 9to5Google · HBO Max help · NuvioTV src

The keyboard can collapse while you browse results

bobsupra's NuvioTVOS hosts its Netflix-style search on the same tvOS .searchable field as its native view: the keyboard "stays mounted for focus restoration but collapses while browsing results", and Up from the filter chips brings it back. It also tells the Grid keyboard from the Linear one by view size (over 320 pt tall and under 900 pt wide). This is the mechanism a Stage Search would need.

bobsupra/NuvioTVOS src, beta 3.4.1 (5 Oct)

Filters: pills with values, a count, a Clear

VortX's Discover puts a "Filters (n)" chip beside the type, catalog and genre chips; a glass sheet holds include/exclude genres, decades, upcoming only, age rating, runtime and season count, with "N shown · Clear filters" and pages loaded until 18 titles match. Plex's August 2026 tvOS build "refined the browse page header with more compact filters and a simplified item count".

VortX src · Plex beta notes 2026.17.0

Two warnings for our layout

Channels added a filter bar to its tvOS 26 search and found it "interferes" with the Grid keyboard, squishing results and letting them scroll under the bar. A developer forum thread (August 2026, unanswered) reports a page-level .searchable inside a sidebar TabView being overlapped by the system sidebar button on tvOS 26. Our rail is custom, so the second one is a watch item, not a blocker.

Channels community · Apple Developer Forums 843158

Also checked, nothing to port

  • tvOS 27 (14 September) changed nothing in search or the keyboard. Apple's HIG still says the Siri Remote shows the linear keyboard; the Grid stays a user setting that only Apple's apps can slide away.
  • Official NuvioTV Android pushes title completions into the keyboard's suggestion strip instead of drawing its own list, keeps old rows while typing, and uses skeleton rows only when there is nothing yet. Its Discover is three dropdowns and a grid ending in a Load more card, with no hero.
  • Stremio's Discover opens a side preview panel on selection (logo, art, year, runtime, synopsis, trailer). Android TV 1.9.x in 2026 touched only the player.
  • Infuse 8.5 (July 2026) regrouped search into movies, shows, episodes, collections and people. Filters in search are still an open request there.
  • Android TV's own quality guidelines (May 2026) dropped the in-app search requirement in favour of the Engage SDK and ask for a visible mic beside the field. Not applicable to tvOS, but it shows where Google thinks search is going: into the system.
Worth knowing before choosing

Constraints that shape every option

1 · The keyboard owns the top of the screen while typing

Linear: the field plus a 245 pt band under it, full width. Grid: a 432 × 750 pt block docked left, results to its right. Either way, a stage's text block (logo slot 150, meta 32, synopsis 150) cannot sit under the keyboard. Any stage layout needs a typing state where the stage steps back, and a browsing state where it returns.

2 · The strip fits one row, and only two more are mounted

A grid in the strip would page vertically row by row, which is what the strip does anyway. Rows per genre or per catalog map onto it directly, and the mount window (two rows each side of focus) caps how many catalog requests are live at once. A fan-out over 20 genres costs five requests at a time, not twenty.

3 · The HIG contract already favours pills

Menu { Picker } is the contract's dropdown pattern; it calls horizontal chip rows the old anti-pattern. Library L1 built the List and Sort pills, the smart-filter chips and the count line with a source badge. They are private to LibraryView today and need extracting, which is small. Accent on a selected chip or pill is allowed.

4 · The rail takes 116 pt, and the Grid keyboard another 432

With the rail Always Visible and the Grid keyboard open, two left columns are already on screen. A third (a category list, Netflix-style) does not fit. Designs that add a left column should hide the rail while typing, or place the column on the right.

5 · Shared code already has most of the data

Four Discover empty reasons and four Search ones exist; the UI merges them. TMDB discover filters exist for folders: genre include/exclude, date range, vote average and count, language, country, keywords, companies, networks, watch providers. Missing: runtime and "upcoming only". A person page exists (reached from Detail); person search does not. Add-on search results are neither merged nor ranked.

6 · The stage needs art that Discover has

Catalog items carry posters and usually backdrops; the stage falls back poster → wash when a backdrop is missing, as Home does. Logos come from the same lookup Home uses. A Discover stage inherits Home's "no logo, draw the title" rule unchanged.

O1 · Dressed Search

Keep the page, borrow the stage's clothes

The structure stays as merged: field, keyboard, Recent, Discover under it. What changes is everything the eye reads as Home's. The ambient wash follows the focused poster or result. The three chip rows become one row of pills that show their value, with a context line under it, so the grid starts a whole poster row higher. Results group by type (Top result, Movies, Series, People), merged by id, add-ons ranked metadata first with stream-only add-ons capped, and the row heading uses Home's section title token. The genre is remembered with the catalog. The four empty reasons get four messages.

For

  • The cheapest route to "looks like one app".
  • Nothing moves that the device pass already proved.
  • Everything in it is also the typing state of O2 and O4, so it is never wasted.
  • Matches official NuvioTV, Orivio and bobsupra's Discover controls.

Against

  • Still a flat scrolling page. No stage, no strip paging.
  • A wash behind a keyboard is as far as the stage language goes while typing.
  • Discover stays a grid you scroll, not a place you land.

O2 · Living Search

O1 plus a backdrop and a details block, no strip

The page keeps one scroll, so there is no row paging and no fixed strip. What it gains is VortX's living backdrop: the focused title's art fills the screen, a details block (logo, meta, three synopsis lines) is pinned at a fixed distance above the grid so the two never collide, and the grid tucks under it as you scroll. While typing, the keyboard band covers exactly where the details block was, so the typing state shows backdrop and wash behind grouped rows, as Jellyfin does. Down into results collapses the keyboard and the block comes back for the focused result, with the add-ons that found it.

For

  • Discover gets the stage feel without leaving the Search tab or adding a rail item.
  • A dense grid stays on screen, so it is still fast when you know what you want.
  • Shipped and proven in VortX, the app closest to ours.

Against

  • Not the Stage. Different swap timing, no strip, a scroll that can drift. Two "stage-like" systems to keep in sync.
  • The details block and the keyboard band fight for the same 300 pt; with the Grid keyboard the block has to move right or hide.
  • Relies on collapsing the keyboard while browsing, which bobsupra does but Apple does not document.

O3 · Stage Discover, flat Search My pick, with O1

Discover becomes a place; Search stays a tool

The split VortX and Orivio both arrived at, built from our own parts. Discover leaves the bottom of the Search page and becomes a stage-and-strip page of its own: the stage shows the focused title exactly as Home does, and the strip pages one row per genre of the selected type and catalog ("Sci-Fi · Cinemeta", "Thriller · Cinemeta"). Catalogs without genres give one row per catalog. Type and catalog pills live in the top band where the folder page already keeps its Edit band; a Grid pill opens today's grid for the current row (See All exists). Where it lives is a setting, as in official NuvioTV: a Discover item on the rail and tab bar, or a Discover row under the Search field that opens it, or off (UX-8 kept). Search itself takes O1.

For

  • Reuses the folder page almost whole: wash, StageView, StageController, StripPager, swap timing. Rows are CatalogRowView.
  • Exactly Home's motion and geometry, so it reads as the same app without a second system.
  • Browsing by genre is one press per genre; this is Genius Browse's and HBO Max's shape.
  • Search stays flat, which is what every app with a stage does.
  • The mount window caps genre fan-out at five live requests.

Against

  • One row of posters on screen instead of a grid. Fourteen genres is fourteen pages down.
  • A sixth rail item if Discover gets its own entry; the bar is at five today.
  • Genre rows need the genre extra; catalogs without it fall back to one row per catalog, which can be short.
  • Filters beyond genre (decade, rating) have no natural home in a genre-row model; they would live on the Grid view.

O4 · Stage Search

One stage page for both states

The 4 October board's S3, refined by this round. The Search tab is a stage page. Before typing, the strip pages Recent, Browse tiles (genres, decades, your collection folders) and Trending rows, or O3's genre rows, and the stage follows focus. The field is a glass pill at the top-left of the stage. Selecting it drops the system keyboard; the stage dims to the wash and grouped rows fill the space under the band. Down into results folds the keyboard back into the pill (bobsupra's mechanism), the stage returns with the focused result and the add-ons that found it, and the strip pages Top result, Movies, Series, People. Up from row 0 brings the keyboard back.

For

  • One tab, one page, one language with Home. Discover never needs its own entry.
  • Same-name titles are told apart on the stage (1984 and 2021, a film and its series).
  • The background trailer works here too.

Against

  • Two states to keep straight, with the focus choreography between them. Every bug class the home stage had, plus a keyboard.
  • Fewer results on screen than O1 while browsing, so slower when you know the title.
  • With the Grid keyboard the stage text has no left side to sit on; it needs a right-anchored variant or must hide.
  • Nobody ships this. The keyboard fold is observed in one fork, not documented by Apple.

Mix and match

Pieces that fit any of the four

Each is small on its own. The first three are the ones every option assumes.

Results by type, merged and ranked

Top result, Movies, Series, Collections, People. Duplicates merged by id. Metadata add-ons first, stream-only add-ons last and capped (Orivio: 40 per catalog, 8 per stream-only add-on). The finding add-ons named on the stage or under the focused card. Per-add-on rows stay as a setting, with the add-on's name in the header.

One pill bar for Discover

Type ▾ · Catalog ▾ · Genre ▾ (· Filters (n)), each a Menu { Picker }, plus a context line. Library L1's pills extracted and shared. Remember the genre as well as the catalog. This alone frees a poster row on O1.

People from TMDB

Round portraits in a People row, opening the person page we already have. Apple's guidance, Infuse 8.5, Swiftfin, Jellyfin and HBO Max all do it. Needs a TMDB person search call; the key is bundled.

Suggestion chips

The typed text in quotes first, then title completions from the add-ons' search catalogs, up to 8. The system searchSuggestions API draws plain text rows; VortX uses it. Chips with posters are ours to draw.

Filters sheet

VortX's shape: include/exclude genres, decade range, minimum rating, upcoming only, runtime, "N shown · Clear", and more pages loaded until 18 titles match. With TMDB as a source, most fields exist in shared code today; runtime and upcoming do not.

Discover placement setting

Off / Under Search / Own tab, as official NuvioTV ships it. "Own tab" adds Discover to the rail and tab bar. Hide Discover (UX-8) becomes the Off value, so nobody loses what they set.

Four empty states, not one

"None of your add-ons can search", "All search sources are off" with a Settings link, "No results for ‘dnue’" with the query shown, and a manifest failure with Retry. The reasons already exist in shared code.

Before typing

Pick one for the idle page: Recent + Discover (today), Browse tiles for genres, decades and collections (Apple TV app, Google TV), a Trending grid (Orivio), or a genre list (Netflix). O3 makes this question smaller: the idle Search page only needs Recent and a Discover entry.

Header with a count

"Discover · 100 loaded" or "38 shown" beside the pills, Plex's August 2026 compact header. L1's count line already formats it.

Menu goes to the top first

In a long grid, Menu scrolls back to the first poster; a second Menu leaves (Orivio, bobsupra). The strip already pages to row 0 on Menu.

The one thing every option has to survive

For you to overrule

Recommendation

O3 with O1, in two steps. Search stays the flat system-field page S1 already is, dressed in the stage's wash, type and pills, with results grouped by type and a People row. Discover leaves the bottom of that page and becomes a stage-and-strip page built from the folder page, one row per genre, with Type and Catalog pills in the top band and a Grid pill for density. Where Discover lives becomes a setting.

1
Dressed Search (O1). Wash, pill bar with the genre remembered, grouping and ranking, People, suggestion chips, four empty states. Small, it lands as the S1-b batch the Search plan already outlines, and every line of it is also O4's typing state.
2
Stage Discover (O3). A DiscoverRowsPage from FolderRowsPage, rows per genre, the placement setting, the Grid pill onto today's grid. It waits for the Home Stage & Strip batch to merge, because it reuses its views.

Why not O4 first. Nobody ships a keyboard over a stage, and the one mechanism that makes it work is observed in a fork rather than documented. Build O3's stage on Discover, live with it for a beta, then decide whether Search wants the same treatment. If it does, O4 is O1's typing state plus O3's stage and a fold; nothing built before it is thrown away. Why not O2. It is a second stage system with its own timing and scroll, next to the one Home just got; keeping two in step costs more than it saves.

What this does to density. O3 trades a 7-wide grid for one row per genre, which is slower for "show me everything popular". The Grid pill keeps today's grid one press away for exactly that case, and it is where decade and rating filters would go.

Your calls

Questions only you can answer

1 · Does Discover get its own rail and tab item? It makes six items. The alternatives are a Discover row under the Search field that opens the stage page, or both, chosen in Settings.
2 · Rows per genre, or a paged grid in the strip? Genre rows match Home and Genius Browse. A grid in the strip pages seven posters at a time and keeps density, but reads as a different component.
3 · Group search results by type by default? Merged and ranked (Orivio, VortX, Infuse, Apple), with per-add-on rows as the setting, or the other way round.
4 · TMDB as a Discover source now? The key is bundled and the filters exist for folders. It is what makes decade, rating and exclusion filters possible; add-on catalogs cannot do them.
5 · People from TMDB? A People row needs one new call and opens the existing person page.
6 · What sits on the idle Search page? Recent plus a Discover entry only (O3), or Browse tiles, or a Trending grid.
7 · Hide the rail while typing? With the Grid keyboard open, the rail and the keyboard are two left columns. Hiding the rail during typing keeps the results at full width.
