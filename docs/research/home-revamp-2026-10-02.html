NuvioTV Home Revamp

This board compares NuvioTV's Home screen with the official Nuvio apps, the other Apple TV ports of Nuvio, VortX, and the big tvOS apps (Apple TV app, Netflix, Prime, Disney+, Infuse, Plex, Swiftfin, Arctic Fuse). It sets out four directions for the Home screen and a set of smaller pieces you can mix in, ending with a recommendation. Each direction has a rough mockup, what it fixes, and what it costs.

Starting point

NuvioTV Home today

Two Home layouts behind one switch

  • Classic (default): a full-bleed hero carousel (up to 8 titles, 8 s timer, page dots, CTA) that scrolls away with the rows. Section titles sit above the rows.
  • Pinned (Nuvio-Style Hero ON, or Show Hero OFF): the hero is fixed at the top and shows the focused card after 0.2 s. Rows scroll underneath, with their titles drawn inside an extended focus band.
  • Continue Watching (16:9 cards) comes first, then Upcoming, then the catalog and collection rows in your order. Rows show 18 items and end with See All.
  • Posters come in Small, Medium, Medium+ and Large, with three corner styles, Hide Titles, Landscape Rows, Card Depth, an accent ring, No Zoom, poster-colour options, Row Edge Fade and OLED black.
  • Navigation is the system top tab bar or a sidebar revealed with Menu.

What testers keep reporting

  • Row titles bounce, settle late or hide under posters (BUG-87, 88, 89). The fix merged 10-02 and Steven hasn't confirmed it yet.
  • Short top rows park low and jump; late-loading rows jump (BUG-121, 122).
  • Stutter between rows. The whole Home body re-renders about twice per row hop (BUG-126).
  • Up from row 1 doesn't bring the hero back (BUG-112, 114). The tab bar never scrolls away (BUG-66).
  • Cards clipped by zoom, and depth effects too subtle to see (BUG-92, 93, 110).
  • Asks: browse collections like the Apple TV app (FEAT-36), Home-style collection pages (FEAT-43), a size between Medium and Large (FEAT-39, shipped as Medium+).

What else he said

  • Keep: Depth Takes Poster Color ("WOW"), the current type, the tab bar hiding on scroll.
  • Cold, dull, too dark: Fusion paints a bright blurred colour wash from the focused title. NuvioTV is near-black.
  • Blurry on 4K: every image is decoded at 1920 px or less, but a 4K Apple TV draws 3840. Any new full-bleed layer must decode at 3840 px, and posters need w780.
  • Trailer morph: the ring loses the poster colour, a ghost logo appears when you move off a card whose morph has just started, and trailers stop altogether until relaunch after an Infuse hand-off.
  • Collections should look and move exactly like Home.
Worth knowing before choosing

Most of the Home code fights the focus engine

On tvOS the focus engine decides where a row lands when focus moves. NuvioTV's pinned layout keeps a title above a row that the engine is free to park anywhere, so it measures where the row ended up and nudges it back. Nearly all of the bugs above live in that loop.

Amber = code that exists only to keep pinned rows and their titles in place. About 22k lines across all Home-adjacent files.

Tuning knobs

  • 45 hero size constants in the theme, 20 of them for pinned-row rests, reaches and slack (e.g. heroPinnedRowSettleMaxNudge 220, heroPinnedRowsDeviceParkSlack 96).
  • About 28 Home debug.* switches, 5 live A/B toggles in About and 11 probes.
  • Every one of these exists because a tester's photo showed a row landing in the wrong place on his TV and not in the simulator.

Why it matters for the revamp: the forks that avoided this (VortX and official NuvioTV's Modern layout) put the rows in a fixed-height strip at the bottom of the screen, so a row can only ever land in one place. Directions B and C below take that route, which would let most of the amber code go.

Patterns that keep coming up

  • Resume first: Continue Watching is the first row or is the hero itself.
  • A clearlogo, meta line and short synopsis bottom-left over a scrim.
  • The backdrop follows focus with a debounce (0.45–0.5 s), never on every press.
  • Trailers only after the viewer stops moving (2–7 s), muted, and torn down on any move.
  • Different card shapes per row type: 16:9 for resume and episodes, posters for catalogs.
  • Labels on tiles: New, Season Finale, #1, progress.
  • Users choose the layout, often on first launch with a preview.

Anti-patterns

  • Rows that move after the viewer has stopped. Nobody else re-centres a row after it has landed.
  • A hero whose timer changes the backdrop while you're in a row.
  • Decoding a full-size backdrop on every focus change, and several previews playing at once.
  • Deep navigation behind a top bar (Plex rolled it back).
  • Home changing under the viewer because a late sync reorders rows.

A · Fusion-style Page Chosen 2026-10-03

Fusion's layout (the app Steven uses as his reference), close to the Apple TV app's. A full-bleed carousel sits at the top and scrolls away with the page. Below it come Continue Watching and the rows: a heading above each row, and each title's name under its poster. Every Down press is one native page scroll, and nothing moves after it settles. Behind the rows, a blurred full-colour wash taken from the focused title fades in after a short pause and shows what's in focus, instead of an info panel. Trailers play in that background layer or in the row, chosen in a setting.

For

  • It matches what Steven measured in Fusion: one ~1 s ease-out per press, where NuvioTV makes four movements over about 2 s.
  • It retires the pinned-row correction code (~5,000 lines) and its bug class. Steven already found the unpinned classic mode smoother.
  • The colour wash answers "cold, dull, too dark" and builds on the poster-colour effect he loves.
  • Collection folders can use the same page component (FEAT-43).

Against

  • No synopsis for the focused title while you browse the rows. It stays in the carousel and on Detail.
  • The wash must stay cheap: one 3840 px decode per pause, never one per press.

B · the top half never moves. Up and Down page whole rows through the bottom strip.

B · Stage & Strip My first pick, before Steven's video

Official NuvioTV's Modern layout and VortX's living backdrop. The top ~54% is a stage that can't take focus. It shows the focused title (art on the right, logo, meta and synopsis on the left) and swaps after a 0.45 s pause. The bottom ~46% is a strip that shows one row at a time, with the next row's title peeking below. Up and Down page whole rows, so each row lands in exactly one place. The row title sits at the top of the strip, outside the focus band.

For

  • It's what Steven keeps holding up as the reference (his official-Nuvio video frame looks like this).
  • A row can only land in one place, so the bounce, late-settle, short-row and title-clearance bugs can't happen. Most of the amber code and 20 constants can go.
  • Keeps the info panel people like.

Against

  • Only one row is visible at a time, so browsing many catalogs means more presses. Grid layout or Landscape Rows help.
  • A rewrite of the row container: HomeView's body and scrolling, not the cards.
  • Steven has spent a month tuning the current pinned layout with you, so it needs a careful explanation and a way back.

Orivio's default. At the top of Home, a big rolling carousel (the Top 10 or your hero sources) owns the screen. Press Down and it shrinks into B's stage, which follows focus, with the rows in the strip. Up from the first row brings the carousel back.

Decided 2026-10-03

Direction chosen

Hero model: A · Fusion-style page. A carousel at the top that scrolls away with the page, and native scrolling below with one motion per press. The pinned hero, its focus-following and the row correction code are retired.

Ambient background: yes, on by default. A blurred full-colour wash from the focused title behind the page, cross-fading after a short pause, decoded at 3840 px. A setting turns it off for OLED black.

Titles: a section heading above each row, plus each title's name under its poster (Hide Titles still turns the names off). The heading scrolls with its row and is never tracked separately.

Trailers: both, as a setting. Trailer Location becomes Background or In Row.

  • Background: plays behind the page after the viewer rests, and stops on any move.
  • In Row: today's morph, started only once the row has rested, keeping the poster-colour ring.

Collections: folders are a second instance of the same page component (FEAT-43). The folder's title or logo is a header that rises gently on scroll, and the grid view stays as an option.

Today's fix batch (docs/steven-beta19-rc1-verdict-batch-plan-2026-10-02.md):

  • Drops M2 (one-motion settle rules), M5 (hero cross-fade) and N1 (Up into Genres). The new page makes them unnecessary.
  • Keeps everything that survives the redesign:
    • the trailer server wedge (B)
    • 4K decode and w780 posters (I1)
    • edge fade (F)
    • morph and ring fixes (M3, R1, R2)
    • tab bar snap (T1)
    • collection header (C)
    • trailer delay setting (M4)
    • Auto-Play ranking (A)

Rules for the new page:

  • One motion per press, and nothing moves after it settles.
  • Home rests at offset 0 at the top, so the tab bar is never half shown.
  • Every full-bleed image decodes at 3840 px.
  • The edge fade belongs to the row component, so it appears wherever rows do.

Next step

Write an implementation plan for the new page, the same way as the Detail + Settings plan (specs, waves, gates, device pass in the Test profile). It runs on the Mac after the fix batch lands, since both touch HomeView, BrowseComponents and InlineTrailerCard.
