## P1 (fix before the device pass)

1. **`MPVPlayerView.swift:1549` (and `:353`)**: the click guard for the light tap is stamped only for Select/Play-Pause. An arrow click on the Siri Remote's touch surface (gen-1 edge clicks; the gen-2+ clickpad and ring) is also a touch, so `handleLightTap` probably also fires on Left/Right/Up/Down clicks. During a pill walk or a step seek with the bar up, that flips the right label to "ends …" and back on every click. A long Select hold whose touch-up lands > 0.5 s after the press began gets through too. **Fix:** stamp `lastClickUptime` for every press type in `pressesBegan` and again in `pressesEnded` (the tap recognises at touch-up), and ignore the tap while any press is down. Confirm on the device pass (item: light tap vs pill walk).

## P2 (fix before merge)

2. **`MPVPlayerView.swift` (no `pressesCancelled` override; `pressesEnded` `:1661`)**: a cancelled Left/Right press never reaches `pressesEnded`. That happens on Home/TV button or Siri mid-hold, or when a recognizer claims the press. The hold timer then keeps ticking, `mode` stays `.stepping` (the bar can never hide: `mayHide` is false), and the next release commits a preview that has run on, up to the EOF clamp. A cancelled Menu goes to `super`. That is the path the UI legs see on the simulator ("`pressesCancelled` .menu right after `pressesBegan` consumed it", then the cover dismisses). **Fix:** override `pressesCancelled`. Arrows → `stopHoldTimer(); apply(transport.cancel())` (no commit). Menu → swallow when `swallowMenuRelease` (clear it), otherwise `super`. Re-run `testScanMenuCancelsBackToOrigin` / `testMenuHidesBarThenExits` afterwards: this may be the simulator failure's cause.

3. **`MPVPlayerView.swift:1758`, `:1777`, `:1789`, `:1909`**: slow uncached commit (`[.keyframes, .exact]`, exactly the case where the seek takes > 1.5 s on a debrid HTTP stream). The exact-stage deadline and `commitLandWork` both fire at 1.5 s. The deadline issues the exact seek on top of the in-flight keyframes seek, and `issueSeek` resets `startedSeekGeneration = nil`. The keyframes restart is then dropped, so the keyframes picture never shows, and the commit's landing handback (`generation == commitGeneration`, the keyframes generation) never matches. The preview clears by timer while the real position is still the origin, so the played fill snaps back to the origin until the exact seek lands (the spec's "does not jump back while the seek lands"). Rarer: if the keyframes `MPV_EVENT_SEEK` arrives after the reset, it is attributed to the exact generation. The planner then completes on the keyframe landing and never learns the exact landing (class 5; an intro that starts between the keyframe and the target could then auto-skip after a deliberate seek). **Fix:** in `runExactStage` set `commitGeneration` to the exact stage's generation and re-arm `commitLandWork` (e.g. 3 s). Preferably, let the deadline wait for the keyframes `SEEK` event before it stacks the second seek, rather than firing it blind at 1.5 s.

4. **`iosApp/NuvioTVUITests/PlayerTransportUITests.swift:115`, `:249`, `:252`**: vacuous legs. `testScanMenuCancelsBackToOrigin` calls `XCTExpectFailure(strict: false)` at test scope, so every assertion in the leg is non-fatal and the leg passes whatever happens: scan start, speed back to 1.0, return to the origin, player still up. `testMenuHidesBarThenExits` wraps both Menu-precedence assertions the same way. The batch's riskiest rule (Menu precedence, scan cancel) has no automated proof. **Fix:** scope the expectation to the single "player still presented" assertion, or drop it after #2. Extract the `.menu` decision into a pure `MenuPrecedence.resolve(mode:upNextShowing:pillFocused:barUp:)` and unit-test the five rows. Keep both on the device-pass list until then.

## P3 (nice to have)

5. `MPVPlayerView.swift:1754`: the keyframes `onRejected` sets `pendingExact = nil` unconditionally. Guard on `pendingExact?.generation == gen` so a late rejection can't drop a newer commit's exact stage.
6. `MPVPlayerView.swift:1841`: the Menu-cancel return seek passes `fromSec: cachedProps().position` with kind `.user`. `seekCompleted` then records scan-end→origin, and `intervalsAtSeekPositions` marks the interval at the scan END as deliberate. The spec says no span (that interval must still auto-skip when played). Pass `fromSec: target`.
7. `iosApp/NuvioTVTests/SkipSegmentPlannerTests.swift:488` `testRecordUserSpanConsumesScannedIntervals`: it passes because of the follow-up seek landing at 120. `recordUserSpan(0, 300)` alone consumes nothing for `[100, 190)`, since `intervalsAtSeekPositions` checks endpoints only. Assert straight after `recordUserSpan` with an endpoint inside the interval, and rename it (it is "endpoint", not "scanned through").
8. `MPVPlayerView.swift:1812`: a Scan-mode hold while paused latches `.scanning` at 2× on a paused core. Nothing moves, Right "cycles" silently, and the bar can't hide until Select. Either unpause for the scan (restoring the pause on Menu), or step when paused.
9. `MPVPlayerView.swift:1533`: a press that revives an auto-hidden chip (Left/Right) republishes the prompt, then the next tick (seek in flight → `Decision()`) hides it again: a one-tick chip flash on every seek press out of an intro. Skip the immediate `updateSkipPrompt` for arrow presses, or reveal only after the seek lands.
10. `MPVPlayerView.swift:1820`: `scanRestore` is set by an async main hop. A scan ended before the hop lands uses the fallback `(state.playbackSpeed, false)` and then gets the stale value written back afterwards. Harmless today (no mute control). Capture the restore on main before dispatching.
11. `MPVPlayerView.swift:1932`: the hide delay is fixed when the timer is armed, and the work item never re-reads `paused`. A pause that arrives without input (programmatic) hides the bar at 4 s even with the pause card off. Re-check `TransportHideRule.delay` when the timer fires.
12. `NativePlaybackCoordinator.swift:689`: a native retry honours `startOver` over `resumeAtSec`, while mpv (`computeResumePosition`) prefers `resumeAtSec`. A failed Start Over at 5:00 resumes at 5:00 on mpv but restarts on native. Pick one rule.
13. `StreamPickerView.swift:1062`: the failed link goes into `RejectedStreamLinks` (8 h) before the other-engine retry. A retry that works but is exited before 300 s leaves a working link flagged. Skip the reject when the alert offers a retry engine, or clear it when the retry starts.
14. `SettingsViewModel.swift:470`: the new properties sit between `enhancedRenderer`'s doc comment and its declaration, so the comment now describes `holdMode`.
15. `Localizable.xcstrings`: 22 keys appended unsorted with no trailing newline (Xcode will rewrite: churn in the next commit). About 25 strings are English-only (five locales owed, as planned). `PlayerPills.swift` `accessibilityValue("focused")` is an English literal read by VoiceOver.
16. `PlayerTransportBar.swift:112`: `endClock` allocates a `DateFormatter` per render. Cache one per locale.
17. `PlayerScreen.swift` `annotated(_:)`: correctness depends on the representable capturing the closure at make time. A future `updateUIViewController` that refreshes the closure would re-enable the native retry after a forced-mpv failure (the alert would alternate engines). Gate on `context.forcedEngine == nil` explicitly.
18. `SeekProbe.swift`: `Int(probe.lastTarget)` traps on a non-finite target (DEBUG only). Use `String(format: "%.0f")`.
